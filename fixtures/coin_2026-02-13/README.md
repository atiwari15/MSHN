# Fixture: coin_2026-02-13

Coinbase furnished its Q4'25 shareholder letter on an Item 2.02 8-K after the close on 2026-02-12, and the stock rose **~16.5%** the next session ($141.09 → $164.32) after four straight down days into the print.

This is the **inverse of the NVDA trap**. There, headline numbers were excellent and the stock fell. Here the sequential numbers got *worse* — total revenue $1.8B (down 5% Q/Q), transaction revenue $983M (down 6% Q/Q), operating expenses up 9% Q/Q — and the stock rallied hard. A system that reads only the headline quarter concludes the stock should have dropped, or that there's no catalyst at all.

The filing-grounded positive case is elsewhere in the same letter: full-year 2025 revenue up 9% Y/Y, $11.3B in cash and equivalents, $1.7B of Class A stock repurchased, and fully diluted share count down 8% Q/Q to 268 million. The honest answer also notes that the filing alone doesn't fully account for a 16.5% move — the rest is expectations and positioning, which no SEC filing contains.

It is the fixture set's **up move with a genuine catalyst**.
