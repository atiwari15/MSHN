# Fixture: nvda_2025-04-16

After the close on 2025-04-15, NVIDIA filed an Item 8.01 8-K disclosing that the U.S. government had informed it on April 9 that exporting H20 integrated circuits to China (including Hong Kong and Macau) and D:5 countries now requires a license, made indefinite on April 14, and that Q1 FY2026 results would include **up to approximately $5.5 billion** of charges for H20 inventory, purchase commitments, and related reserves. The stock fell **~6.9%** the next session ($112.20 → $104.49).

This fixture exists to test a **non-earnings catalyst**. The other catalyst fixtures are earnings releases where the substance lives in EX-99 exhibits full of revenue and margin figures; this filing has **no exhibits at all** — just one short regulatory disclosure in the 8-K body. It checks that retrieval and explanation aren't quietly tuned to earnings-shaped language.

Run `python fixtures/fetch.py nvda_2025-04-16` to download the filing.
